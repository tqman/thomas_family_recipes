After Action Report:

Turkey: peaked at about 155F, over target. Turned it down to 170F after the
first hour and watched it; turned the oven off at about 145F and it still
peaked at 155F. Next year: turn the oven OFF at about 135F, let it peak,
then 170F to hold.

Potatoes: the recipe is for Yukon Gold (knockoffs) and it still needs the
full hour at 400F. 45 minutes was undercooked and they wouldn't rice.
(Meg's save: bowl, a little water, plastic wrap with vent holes, 4 minutes
in the microwave.)

Shopping Notes:
- You need 7C (56 oz) of cream:
   4C for mashed potatoes x4 (was 2C for x2 in 2025)
   2C for creamed onions x2
   1C for brussels sprouts braised in cream
- Mashed potatoes x4 also means 8 lbs Yukon Golds, 4 sticks butter, 8 heads garlic
- You need 32 oz of cheddar (for quadruple mac and cheese)
- You need 10 oz of Gruyere (for quadruple mac and cheese)
- Don't forget chives for the creamed onions!
- Whipped cream for pies: just buy a can (2026)
