# Small Vanilla Cake (6-inch)

Makes a two-layer 6" cake. Butter for flavor, a little oil so the crumb stays moist a day later — handy when you're baking the layers ahead and icing the next day.

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1½ C (195g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ C (120ml) milk
- 1 tsp vinegar
- ¼ C (55g) unsalted butter, room temp
- 1 C (200g) granulated sugar
- ¼ C (60ml) vegetable oil
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract

### Vanilla Buttercream

- 1 C (220g) unsalted butter, room temp
- 3 C (360g) powdered sugar
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2–3 Tbsp milk

## Instructions

### Cake

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease and line the bottoms of two 6" round pans.
2. Stir the milk and vinegar together in a measuring cup; set aside to curdle while you make the batter.
3. Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl.
4. Beat the butter and sugar on medium-high until pale and fluffy.
5. Mix in the oil until smooth. Scrape the bowl, mix again.
6. Mix in the eggs and vanilla.
7. Add half the dry ingredients, mix on low; just before it's fully absorbed, pour in the milk.
8. Scrape the bowl, mix in the rest of the dry ingredients.
9. Divide between the two pans. Bake 30–35 min, until a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
10. Cool in the pans 20 min, then turn out onto a rack and cool completely before frosting.

### Buttercream

1. Beat the butter smooth.
2. Add the sugar one cup at a time, with 1 Tbsp milk between each cup (start with 2 Tbsp total; add the 3rd only if needed). Mix in the vanilla last.
3. Once the cakes are fully cool, level the domes if needed and spread buttercream between the layers.
4. Thin crumb coat over the whole cake, chill ~20 min.
5. Final coat once the crumb coat firms up. Decorate.

## Notes

Bake-ahead: bake and fully cool the layers, wrap airtight, and make the buttercream fresh at assembly time. Day-old layers actually ice cleaner than fresh. Weigh the flour if you can — 195g beats a packed cup, and small cakes punish a heavy scoop.
